Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026-10-28

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose and protect your personal information when you communicate with us through Amazon messaging, purchase our products or use our customer chat support.

We may collect personal information you voluntarily provide to us, including your name, shipping address, email address, Amazon order information, phone number, and the content of your chat messages when you contact our customer support.

We use your personal information only for:

- Sending order updates and after-sales support communications;
- Complying with applicable legal requirements.

We will NOT sell, rent or share your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Your Rights (California Residents / CCPA)
If you are a California resident, you have the right to request access to or deletion of your personal information that we hold. You may submit your request by sending us a message via Amazon Buyer-Seller Messaging.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

4. Data Security
We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss or misuse.

5. Data Retention
We retain your information only as long as necessary to resolve your support case, complete the order, and satisfy legal or record-keeping obligations.

6. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via Amazon buyer-seller messages.
